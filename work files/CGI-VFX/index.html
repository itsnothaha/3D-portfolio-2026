For each project, I choose the workflow and tools that best fit its creative and technical requirements rather than following a fixed pipeline.

Blender is my primary tool for 3D modeling, animation, lighting and rendering. I use CLO 3D for clothing and fabric simulations, ZBrush for more organic and detailed forms, and Houdini for particles, fluids, procedural animation and complex simulations.

For texturing, I primarily use Substance 3D Painter, but I also create materials directly in Blender when a project does not require a complex texturing pipeline. I have also worked in Cinema 4D with Arnold and Redshift.

For tracking, I use SynthEyes and sometimes experiment with custom solutions directly in Blender. After Effects and DaVinci Resolve are part of my workflow for compositing, editing, colour correction and final post-production.

My workflow is flexible and constantly evolving. I focus on finding the most efficient and visually effective solution for each project, while using new tasks as an opportunity to explore software, techniques and production methods.
